Frequently asked questions

Is my financial data private?

Yes. Dueline stores everything on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

How does iCloud sync work?

When you're signed in to iCloud, Dueline automatically backs up and syncs your data to your private iCloud database, so it restores on a new device or after reinstalling. If you're not signed in, the app works fully offline and keeps data only on that device.

Will I get reminders before bills are due?

Yes. With your permission, Dueline schedules local notifications to remind you before each bill's due date. These reminders are generated entirely on your device.

Can I track recurring bills and income?

Absolutely. Bills and income can be set to repeat on a schedule, so you enter them once and Dueline keeps the upcoming occurrences up to date for you.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting an item in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. To remove synced data, delete the app’s data from your iCloud storage settings on your device.

Which devices are supported?

Dueline is built for iPhone. Your data syncs across your devices through your own iCloud account.
